Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Absolute Local Privacy

SiteAsset Pro runs 100% locally on your machine within Chrome. No images, SVGs, media files, or scraped asset URLs are ever transmitted to any external server, analytics platform, or cloud service.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, sell, or monitor any personal data, browsing history, or asset downloads. Everything is processed in-memory in your local browser sandbox and downloaded directly to your device.

3. Permissions

SiteAsset Pro requests minimal Chrome extension permissions solely to inspect the current active tab's DOM for images, vector SVGs, and CSS backgrounds when you click the extension icon.
